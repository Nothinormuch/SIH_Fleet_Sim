⚙️ Decentralized Recovery Mechanics

How the autonomous fleet resolves degraded states in plain words

Autonomous
1
Crashed Winner Lease Expiry (20s TTL)

When a robot crashes after winning an auction, its local claim lease expires autonomously. The unfinished task immediately returns to the unallocated catalog for surviving peers to bid on.

2
Physical Obstacle Sensing

The failed chassis does not disappear; onboard LiDAR sensors register it as a physical obstacle at Layer 0 (50 Hz), preventing any collision without needing network coordination.

3
Anonymous Blocked Cell Replanning

Persistent stationary detections become expiring blocked cells in local occupancy grids, allowing surviving AMRs to replan A* bypass routes around the disabled unit.

4
Partition Healing via Gossip Sync

When partitioned network zones reconnect, peer task gossip (1.0s period) and lease expiry deterministically repair state views across all nodes in 19.38 seconds.

🛠️ Critical Defects Caught & Resolved by the Campaign

Real runtime race conditions uncovered during stress testing and eliminated prior to release

2/2 Fixed & Tested
1. Corridor-Wave Drift under Packet Loss RACE CONDITION

Under network loss, peers held different unclaimed corridor-wave memberships when completion gossip arrived out of order. This caused desynchronization in unidirectional block directions.

Fix: Active claims now maintain their admitted phase, and unclaimed wave membership is re-derived deterministically from the canonical smallest unfinished task ID. Backed by seed-specific regression tests.
2. Empty-Approach Conflict at 20% Loss RESOLVED (2/12 → 12/12)

At 20% loss, a task could be awarded before an empty cross-corridor approach opposed an active loaded wave, creating deadlocks where the failing seed finished only 2 of 12 tasks.

Fix: Cross-corridor work is no longer awarded before approach. After lease convergence and fleet quiescence, one elected idle bidder repositions to the pickup side and joins the next auction. Passing rate jumped from 2/12 to 12/12 (100%).
