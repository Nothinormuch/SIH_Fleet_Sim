6. Layer 3 — Decentralized Contract Net Auction

No central dispatcher. Each idle AMR computes its bid cost, broadcasts over the mesh, and the lowest-cost robot wins via deterministic consensus with expiring lease guarantees.

    participant WMS as WMS Task Injector
    participant A as AMR-A (Idle, Near)
    participant B as AMR-B (Idle, Far)
    participant C as AMR-C (Busy)

    WMS->>A: TASK_NEW (T101: Pick, Drop)
    WMS->>B: TASK_NEW (T101: Pick, Drop)
    A-->>C: Gossip Sync TASK_NEW

    Note over A,C: Local Bidding
    A->>A: Cost = 16.3
    B->>B: Cost = 26.6
    C->>C: Busy: No Bid

    A->>B: BID (T101, Cost: 16.3)
    B->>A: BID (T101, Cost: 26.6)

    Note over A,B: AMR-A Wins (16.3 < 26.6)

    A->>B: AWARD (T101, Owner: AMR-A, TTL: 5s)
    A->>A: State: TO_PICK

    loop Every 2s
        A->>B: AWARD_RENEW (T101)
    end

    A->>B: TASK_DONE (T101)
    B->>C: Gossip TASK_DONE
